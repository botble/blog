# Best Laravel Ecommerce Scripts in 2026 — Top 6 Ranked & Compared

Picking a **Laravel ecommerce script** in 2026 is harder than it should be. CodeCanyon alone lists nearly 250 of them. Half are abandoned, a third are clones of each other, and the rest are split across single-vendor, multivendor, headless, and niche-specific use cases.

We pulled live sales data, demos, and feature sheets for the most popular Laravel ecommerce scripts on the market, then narrowed the field to six that actually ship in production stores today. Every pick below is built on modern Laravel, sells on CodeCanyon, and is backed by an Envato Elite or Power Elite author — the badge Envato gives to authors with proven track records and active support.

Short on time? **MartFury** wins for multi-vendor marketplaces, **FleetCart** wins for traditional single-vendor stores, and **6amMart** wins if you're building food or grocery delivery. Skip to the [comparison table](#comparison-table) for a one-glance view.

## What Is a Laravel Ecommerce Script?

A **Laravel ecommerce script** is a ready-to-deploy online-store application built on the [Laravel framework](https://laravel.com). You buy it once, install it on your hosting, add your products, and start selling — no need to assemble a store from scratch or pay monthly SaaS fees.

It's different from three other things you'll often see in the same search results:

- **Laravel ecommerce package** (Bagisto, LunarPHP, Vanilo) — a library you install into your own Laravel app. You still build the storefront yourself.
- **PHP ecommerce script** — broader category; not all are Laravel. WordPress + WooCommerce dominates this side.
- **SaaS platform** (Shopify, BigCommerce) — hosted-for-you, monthly fee, no source-code access.

Laravel wins for ecommerce when you want **full code ownership**, **modern security defaults**, **a clean codebase your developers can extend**, and **no recurring platform fees**. The scripts below are the path of least resistance — they give you the Laravel advantage without the months of custom build.

## Top 6 Laravel Ecommerce Scripts in 2026

What sets it apart is breadth without bloat. You get 40+ language packs, RTL support, 7+ payment gateways (PayPal, Stripe, Paystack, Razorpay, Mollie, SSLCommerz, plus COD and bank transfer), product variations and specifications, shipping rules, flash sales, coupons, and a built-in affiliate program. For developers, MartFury's value is the **Laravel codebase quality**: PSR-12 compliant, Repository pattern throughout, hookable service providers, and a REST API ready for a custom mobile app. Botble runs an active release cadence — the script is in active development with releases nearly every month.

**Standout features:**

**Best for:** Single-vendor stores wanting a battle-tested codebase with a large reference base.

[FleetCart](https://codecanyon.net/item/fleetcart-laravel-ecommerce-system/23014826) by EnvaySoft (Envato Power Elite) is the most popular **single-vendor** Laravel ecommerce script on CodeCanyon, with nearly 3,000 sales and a 4.60-star rating across 247 reviews. FleetCart focuses on doing single-vendor very well: streamlined product catalog with attributes, options, and variants; modern responsive storefront; SEO-ready product pages with canonical URLs and structured data; tax and tax-zone configuration; coupons; reviews; and a wide payment gateway list (14+ including PayPal, Stripe, Paystack, Razorpay, Authorize.net, bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, Flutterwave, Mercadopago).

If your store is one brand selling its own products, FleetCart is the safe pick. If you need a marketplace where outside sellers list their own products, you'll need to look elsewhere — multivendor isn't part of the script.

**Standout features:**

- 14+ payment gateways included

## Single-Vendor vs Multivendor — Which Do You Need?

Most buyers know what they want here, but if you're on the fence, the decision usually comes down to one question: **are the products being sold yours, or someone else's?**

**Pick single-vendor (FleetCart) if:**
- You manufacture, source, or own the inventory
- You handle fulfillment yourself
- You don't want to manage commission, payouts, or seller approvals
- You want the leanest admin experience

**Pick multivendor (MartFury, Active eCommerce CMS, Shofy, 6amMart, GeniusCart in multi mode) if:**
- Outside sellers list and ship their own products
- You earn revenue via commission, listing fees, or featured placements
- You're building a marketplace, not a store
- You need vendor dashboards, payout management, and store pages

A useful test: if you can describe your business as "we sell X," you're single-vendor. If it's "we connect buyers with sellers of X," you're multivendor. The latter is roughly **2–3× the operational complexity** — go in with eyes open.

## How to Choose the Right Laravel Ecommerce Script

Once you've picked single vs multi-vendor, run candidates through these seven filters before purchase:

1. **Author tier.** Stick to Envato Elite or Power Elite authors — these badges require sustained sales and review thresholds. Every script in this list qualifies.
2. **Update cadence.** Look at the change log on the CodeCanyon page. Active scripts ship multiple updates per quarter. Abandoned ones go silent for 6+ months.
3. **Rating & review count.** Anything below 4.0 ★ deserves scrutiny. A high rating across few reviews is less reliable than 4.5+ over hundreds.
4. **Payment gateway list.** Confirm your region's gateways are supported natively. Local-only gateways (bKash, M-Pesa, MoMo) are make-or-break for some markets.
5. **Language and RTL support.** Selling in MENA, Vietnam, or Latin America? Verify your language is included.
6. **Mobile strategy.** Decide upfront: bundled apps (6amMart, Active eCommerce), API-only with custom client (MartFury, Shofy, FleetCart), or web-only.
7. **License model.** Regular license covers one domain for non-resold use. If you're building a SaaS or selling derivative apps, you need extended.

A common rookie mistake is buying the cheapest script and discovering the gateway you need is a $39 paid add-on. Always check the add-on costs on the author's CodeCanyon profile before purchase.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Laravel good for ecommerce?

Yes. Laravel is the most popular modern PHP framework, with strong security defaults (CSRF, XSS, SQL-injection protection built in), an elegant ORM, and a large ecosystem of ecommerce-ready packages and scripts. It's a particularly strong choice when you want code ownership, want to avoid SaaS monthly fees, or plan to deeply customize the storefront and admin behavior.

### What is the best Laravel ecommerce script in 2026?

MartFury is the best overall for multi-vendor marketplaces — it has the broadest feature set and the cleanest Laravel codebase in the comparison. FleetCart is the best single-vendor script for sales volume and maturity. The "best" choice depends on whether you need marketplace, single-store, or delivery-vertical features.

### What is the cheapest Laravel ecommerce script?

GeniusCart at $35 is the cheapest of the six scripts compared here. Shofy and FleetCart at $59 strike the best balance of price and feature depth.

### Can I use a Laravel ecommerce script for multivendor?

Yes — MartFury, Active eCommerce CMS, Shofy, 6amMart, and GeniusCart all ship with built-in multivendor functionality. FleetCart is the only single-vendor-only option in this list.

### Is there a free Laravel ecommerce script?

The scripts in this comparison are commercial (CodeCanyon, $35–$349). Free open-source alternatives exist as Laravel packages — Bagisto, LunarPHP, and Vanilo are the most popular — but you'll build the storefront and admin yourself. Commercial scripts trade money for time-to-launch.

### Does a Laravel ecommerce script come with a mobile app?

6amMart and Active eCommerce CMS bundle native Flutter apps. MartFury, Shofy, and FleetCart expose REST APIs and leave the app to you (or use a separately-purchased Botble Ecommerce Mobile App). GeniusCart sells its mobile app as a separate add-on.

### How much does a Laravel ecommerce script cost?

Regular licenses range from $35 (GeniusCart) to $349 (MartFury). Most fall in the $59–$99 band. Extended licenses run $159–$999 depending on the script, and are required if you're reselling the script as part of a SaaS or distributed product.

### Do I need to know PHP to use a Laravel ecommerce script?

No, not to use one — every script in this comparison ships with a web-based installer and an admin panel non-technical owners can manage. You only need PHP and Laravel knowledge if you plan to extend the codebase, add custom features, or integrate with proprietary systems.
