## What Is a Laravel Multi-Vendor Marketplace Script?

A **Laravel multi-vendor marketplace script** is a single Laravel codebase hosting many independent sellers under one storefront, with a central admin that manages commission, payouts, vendor approval, and store visibility. Think Amazon, Etsy, or Lazada — one site, thousands of merchants, one operator collecting a cut.

It's structurally different from:

- **Single-vendor scripts** (FleetCart) — one owner, no commission engine.
- **SaaS builders** (Sharetribe, Arcadier) — monthly fees, no source code.
- **Marketplace packages** (Bagisto core, Aimeos) — a library; you build the seller UI.

A ready-made script wires the operator side (admin, payouts, commission) and seller side (dashboard, product upload, withdrawals) together on day one. Why Laravel? Three things it does well for marketplaces — queueable jobs for [Laravel](https://laravel.com) vendor payouts, Eloquent for commission calculations, and a mature ecosystem (Cashier, Horizon, Telescope) for billing edge cases.

## Commission Model Comparison {#commission-models}

The commission engine separates serious marketplace scripts from upgraded single-vendor stores. A flat 10% works for week one. By month six, your top vendor wants 7%, your bottom vendor needs 15%, and electronics needs different math than fashion. The script either handles that or it doesn't.

Five commission models matter in 2026:

- **Flat fee** — fixed dollar amount per order. Predictable, breaks for low-ticket items.
- **Percentage** — fixed % of order value. Industry default.
- **Per-category** — different % per category. Needed for multi-vertical marketplaces.
- **Tiered** — commission changes by sales volume (e.g., 15% under $1k/mo, 10% over). Rewards growth.
- **Per-vendor custom** — override the global rule for individual vendors. Mandatory for negotiated deals.

## Payout & Escrow Comparison {#payouts}

Commission decides what you take. Payouts decide how vendors get the rest. Four payout mechanisms matter in 2026:

- **Stripe Connect** — Stripe's [marketplace payments product](https://stripe.com/docs/connect). Splits the order at checkout automatically. Zero manual reconciliation.
- **Manual bank transfer** — Vendor requests, admin reviews, admin pays. Works everywhere, costs ops time.
- **In-app wallet** — Vendor balance accrues inside the script; payouts come from that wallet.
- **Escrow hold** — Funds held until delivery confirmation, then released. Mandatory in some jurisdictions.

## How to Choose the Right Laravel Multi-Vendor Marketplace Script

Six criteria separate the right script from the wrong one:

1. **Vendor scale.** Shofy and GeniusCart shine for curated 10–100 vendor marketplaces. MartFury and Bagisto handle hundreds. Active eCommerce CMS is the only one with 10,000+ deployments.

2. **Commission flexibility.** Custom rates need per-vendor override (MartFury, 6amMart, Bagisto). Multi-vertical needs per-category (everyone except Shofy). Rewarding volume needs tiered (Active eCommerce CMS).

3. **Payout method.** Manual bank transfers cost ops time per vendor per month. Wallets reduce it. Stripe Connect eliminates it.

4. **KYC depth.** None solve KYC natively. Budget the integration upfront, or retrofit under regulatory pressure later.

5. **Niche fit.** 6amMart is built for delivery. Shofy for fashion/electronics. MartFury is the most generalist. Bagisto is the most extensible.

6. **Open vs commercial.** Bagisto + Marketplace is the only meaningful open-source option. For regulated industries, it's the choice; otherwise commercial scripts win on polish.

Rookie mistake: buying the cheapest script and assuming you can bolt on commission flexibility, KYC, and Stripe Connect later for $200. Those features rebuild the core data model — budget months, not days. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Laravel multi-vendor marketplace script?

A ready-made Laravel application that lets many independent sellers list and sell under one storefront, with a central admin that handles commission, payouts, approvals, and store visibility. Buy the codebase once, host it yourself — no SaaS monthly fees, full code ownership.

### How much does a Laravel multi-vendor marketplace script cost?

Regular CodeCanyon licenses run from $35 (GeniusCart) to $349 (MartFury), with most in the $59–$99 band. Bagisto's core is free but its Marketplace addon is $449. Extended licenses for SaaS resale run $270–$999.

### Can I migrate from a single-vendor store to multi-vendor later?

Sometimes — but painfully. GeniusCart and Shofy ship MV as a toggle. For others, migration means restructuring product, order, and user data models. If you'll need MV within 12 months, start with a multivendor-capable script from day one.

### What's the best open-source Laravel multi-vendor marketplace?

Bagisto with the Webkul Marketplace addon — the only meaningful open-source option here. MIT-licensed core, self-hosted. Trade-off: the addon is paid ($449), and most operators buy additional Webkul extensions, so total cost approaches a commercial script's license.

### How do payouts to vendors work in Laravel marketplaces?

Three patterns. **Manual bank transfer** — vendor requests, admin pays (MartFury, GeniusCart, Bagisto). **In-app wallet** — balance accrues, payouts come from the wallet (Active eCommerce CMS, 6amMart). **Stripe Connect** — splits the payment at checkout (via plugins in MartFury, Shofy, Bagisto). None ship native escrow in 2026.

### Does MartFury support Stripe Connect for vendor payouts?

Yes, but through a marketplace plugin rather than as a native feature. The base script does manual bank/cash payout via withdrawal workflow; Stripe Connect split-payment needs the plugin. Verify with Botble support if automatic checkout settlement is a launch requirement.

### Can vendors have their own custom store URLs and pages?

Yes — every script gives each vendor a profile page with logo, banner, and product listing. MartFury and Bagisto + Marketplace go furthest with store-page builders. Vendor sub-domains or fully custom URLs require additional dev work in all six.

### How long does it take to launch a Laravel multi-vendor marketplace?

MVP timeline: **1–2 weeks** for Shofy, MartFury, GeniusCart; **2–3 weeks** for Active eCommerce CMS; **3–4 weeks** for 6amMart with Flutter apps; **3–6 weeks** for Bagisto + Marketplace. Add 2–4 weeks for KYC, custom commission logic, or Stripe Connect split-payments.
