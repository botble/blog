# Velura: a Spa, Beauty and Nail Salon Website with Booking Built In

I'm Sang from Botble. We just released Velura, a website script for spas, beauty salons and nail studios, and I want to walk through what it actually does.

The reason we built it: most salon websites are a nice-looking brochure with a phone number on it. The booking lives somewhere else, in a scheduling app the owner pays for every month, on a different domain. Velura puts the booking on your own site. A client opens your treatment menu, picks a service, picks a time, and books.

It runs on Laravel 13 and our Botble CMS, so you don't need to write code to use it. Install it, import the demo, then replace the treatments and photos with your own.

## The booking system

This is the part that matters most, so I'll be specific about what it does.

A client picks a treatment, picks a date and time, and can request a particular therapist if they have a favourite. They can leave a note with the booking. The price is copied from the service at the moment they book, so a later price change doesn't rewrite old appointments.

The booking then shows up in your admin panel with a status: pending, confirmed, completed or cancelled. Your front desk works through the list.

Two things to be clear about, because I'd rather you know before you buy than after:

- Clients book an appointment, they don't pay online. There's no card checkout in Velura. You take payment in the salon, the way most salons already do.
- Booking works on a phone properly. That's where most of these bookings come from, so we designed the flow for a small screen first.

## Your treatment menu

The service menu is a real catalog, not a page you hand-edit. Each treatment has a category, a price, an optional sale price, a length in minutes, photos and a description. You can list what's included in the treatment, and mark your signature services as featured so they show up first.

Categories are yours to define. Massages, facials, nails, lashes, hair, whatever you offer.

## Packages and memberships

Two features I pushed for, because repeat clients are the whole business in this industry.

Packages bundle several treatments at one price. Memberships let you sell recurring tiers with their own billing period. Neither is something you have to build yourself.

## Staff, gallery and reviews

Every therapist, stylist and nail artist gets a profile, and clients can ask for them by name when they book. People come back for a person, not just a room.

There's a gallery for your space and your work, client reviews with star ratings, and a blog if you want to write. The blog earns its keep over time, since beauty searches are how a lot of people find a new salon.
