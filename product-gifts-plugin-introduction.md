# Introducing Product Gifts: Boost Sales with Free Gifts for Botble Ecommerce

Product Gifts is a premium plugin for Botble CMS that transforms your promotional strategy with intelligent gift automation. Automatically offer free gift products when customers meet purchase conditions, increasing average order value and customer satisfaction.

Whether you're running seasonal promotions, clearing slow-moving inventory, or rewarding loyal customers, Product Gifts provides all the tools you need to create compelling gift offers that drive sales.

![Product Gifts Dashboard](https://landing.botble.com/product-gifts/images/gift-rules-list.png)

## Why Product Gifts?

Everyone loves free gifts! Here's how Product Gifts can transform your e-commerce store:

- **Increase Average Order Value**: Customers spend more to reach gift thresholds, naturally increasing your revenue per transaction
- **Boost Customer Satisfaction**: Create memorable shopping experiences that keep customers coming back
- **Clear Slow-Moving Inventory**: Offer products that need movement as gifts at order thresholds
- **Fully Automated**: Set up rules once and let the system handle everything - from gift eligibility to order fulfillment
- **Run Effective Campaigns**: Schedule promotions with start/end dates, target specific products or categories

## Key Features

### Flexible Gift Rules

Create unlimited gift promotion rules with customizable conditions. Set minimum order values, schedule promotions with start/end dates, and enable/disable rules instantly from the admin panel.

![Create Gift Rule](https://landing.botble.com/product-gifts/images/create-gift-rule.png)

### Smart Targeting Options

Apply gifts in three different ways:

- **Store-wide**: Gift applies to any order meeting minimum value
- **Specific Products**: Target only selected individual products
- **Product Categories**: Trigger gifts when any product from selected categories is in cart

![Category Targeting](https://landing.botble.com/product-gifts/images/category-targeting.png)

### Multiple Gift Options

Offer multiple gift choices per rule and let customers pick their favorite:

- Define multiple products as gift options for each rule
- Set configurable quantity per gift
- Automatic inventory validation (only shows available products)
- Out-of-stock items are automatically skipped

## How It Works

Getting started with Product Gifts is straightforward:

1. **Create Gift Rules**: Set minimum order values, choose gift products, configure dates, and define targeting conditions in the admin panel

2. **Customer Shops**: When customers add products to cart and meet your conditions, available gifts automatically appear

3. **Select & Checkout**: Customers choose their preferred gift, confirm at checkout, and the gift is automatically added to their order at $0

## Installation Guide

### Requirements

- Botble CMS version 7.6.4 or higher
- PHP version 8.2 or higher
- Active E-commerce plugin (required)
- MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+

### Installation Steps

1. **Download the plugin** from [CodeCanyon downloads page](https://codecanyon.net/downloads)

2. **Extract the downloaded zip file** to your computer

3. **Upload the extracted folder** to `platform/plugins/ecommerce-product-gifts` directory on your server

4. **Go to Admin > Plugins** in your Botble CMS admin panel

5. **Activate the Product Gifts plugin** by clicking the "Activate" button

6. **Navigate to Ecommerce > Product Gifts** to start creating gift rules

After installation, configure your display settings in **Settings > Product Gifts** to match your store's branding.
