## Storefront Features That Convert

### Quick View, Popup Cart & Sticky Add-to-Cart

Shoppers can preview products in a modal, drop them in the cart without a page reload, and check out from a sticky bar that follows them down the page.

### Smart Live Search & Product Filters

As-you-type live search and faceted filters (color, size, brand, price) cut the path from landing to checkout to a handful of taps.

### Color Swatches, Buy Together & Best Deals

Visual variation swatches, "Frequently Bought Together" bundles, and tiered quantity discounts to lift average order value.

### Before & After, Promotional Banners, Countdown Timers

Highlight transformations with before/after sliders, push promos with banner sliders and scrolling text, and create urgency with countdown timers — all configured from the admin panel.

### Mobile-First Experience

Every preset is mobile-first: bottom navigation, mobile menu, in-app-style search, and a swipe-friendly shop cart. Performance stays at A-grade on both desktop and mobile.

### Abandoned-Cart Recovery

Automatically email shoppers who left items in their cart. Recover sales you would otherwise lose.
